Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Relmuru handles information when you use the application. Relmuru is developed and published by Corvin Develop.

1. Overview

Relmuru is a lightweight Windows utility designed primarily for Windows handheld PCs. It uses local Windows functionality to temporarily change display orientation and to register global keyboard shortcuts.

2. Personal information

Relmuru does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or otherwise process personal information for Corvin Develop. Relmuru does not require a user account.

3. Analytics, telemetry, and advertising

Relmuru does not include analytics, telemetry, advertising, behavioral tracking, or in-app purchases. Corvin Develop does not use Relmuru to monitor how you use the application or your device.

4. Local Windows functionality

Relmuru uses local Windows APIs and system functionality to change the current display orientation temporarily and to register global hotkeys. These operations take place on your device and do not require information to be sent to Corvin Develop servers.

5. Locally stored preference

Relmuru may store a small local preference indicating whether you chose not to show the Safety Notice again. This preference remains on your device, does not identify you, and is not transmitted to Corvin Develop or any other server by Relmuru.

6. Network communication

Relmuru does not communicate with Corvin Develop servers to send user information. Its core functionality is local to the Windows device.

7. Data retention and deletion

Because Corvin Develop does not receive personal information from Relmuru, Corvin Develop does not retain personal user data through the application. The small application preference described above is stored locally and can be removed by clearing the application's local data or uninstalling the application, subject to Windows behavior.

8. Children's privacy

Relmuru does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Relmuru's functionality, applicable requirements, or data practices change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, privacy, or support for Relmuru, contact Corvin Develop at corvindevelop@gmail.com.
